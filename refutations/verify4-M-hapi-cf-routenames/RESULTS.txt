E) root cause of D (dcprobe/worker.mjs):
   {"typeofHasSubscribers":"function","valueHasSubscribers":"function hasSubscribers() { [native code] }",
    "plainChannelTypeof":"function",
    "globalScopeRunStores":"throw: Disallowed operation called within global scope. Asynchronou",
    "inHandler":"ok"}
   workerd's node:diagnostics_channel exposes Channel.hasSubscribers as a METHOD, not the
   boolean getter Node.js specifies. orchestrion's injected guard
   `ch.start.hasSubscribers || ch.end.hasSubscribers || ...` is therefore always truthy, so
   every instrumented call enters `channel.start.runStores(...)` even with zero subscribers,
   and runStores is a disallowed global-scope operation on workerd.
