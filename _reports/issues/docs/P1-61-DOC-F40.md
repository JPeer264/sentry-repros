Nothing documents Hapi on Workers. `docs/platforms/javascript/guides/hapi/index.mdx:4-5` sets `sdk: sentry.javascript.node` with `fallbackGuide: javascript.node` and its whole body is one include at `:12`. `docs/platforms/javascript/guides/cloudflare/frameworks/` holds no `hapi.mdx` and the framework list at `guides/cloudflare/index.mdx:16-23` omits Hapi. Separately, `sentryOrchestrionPlugin` and `@sentry/server-utils/orchestrion` appear nowhere in the docs, although `packages/server-utils/package.json:30-62` exports six orchestrion entry points.

Verified on the develop line: `@hapi/hapi` 21 on workerd with `compatibility_date: "2026-05-01"` and `compatibility_flags: ["nodejs_compat", "enable_nodejs_http_server_modules"]`, served through `httpServerHandler` from `cloudflare:node` and wrapped with `Sentry.withSentry()` from `@sentry/cloudflare`. Build-time instrumentation is what makes the framework visible, by either of two public routes: `sentryCloudflareVitePlugin()` from `@sentry/cloudflare/vite` in a Vite build, or `sentryOrchestrionPlugin()` from `@sentry/server-utils/orchestrion/esbuild` when you pre-bundle the Worker yourself and point wrangler's `main` at the output.

Works today:

- Parameterized transaction names (`GET /users/{id}`, source `route`) with nested router and extension spans, per-request isolation, incoming trace continuation, outgoing `fetch` spans with propagation headers, `db` spans from a `mysql` query, `Sentry.logger` logs.

Known limits:

- Errors thrown in a Hapi route handler are not captured on Workers: hapi's `request`/`error` event never fires there, so nothing reaches Sentry.
- A plain `wrangler` build gives neither route names nor database spans; wrangler's own bundler takes no plugins.
- `mysql2` cannot be used on workerd; `mysql` 2.x works.
