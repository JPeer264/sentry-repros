With the snippet as printed and a form posting the listed keys, the throwing action produced no `remix.action_form_data.*` attribute on its span or its event: the instrumentation skips form data when the action errored. The successful action got the keys on its `ACTION` span with the values redacted to `[Filtered]`: the sample names `key_x` and `key_y` contain `key`, which is on the built-in sensitive-key denylist (`packages/core/src/utils/data-collection/filtering-snippets.ts:11`), so their values can never appear. `sendDefaultPii` does not exist on develop; `captureActionFormDataKeys` is an integration-level option that takes precedence over `dataCollection.httpBodies` (`packages/remix/src/utils/formData.ts:16-18`), and without it every field is captured when `httpBodies` includes `incomingRequest`, the default.

- State that the fields land on the action span of successful actions, drop the `sendDefaultPii` sentence, and use sample keys that are not on the built-in denylist, since `key_x` and `key_y` always report `[Filtered]`.
