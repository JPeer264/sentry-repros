Nothing documents NestJS on Workers. `docs/platforms/javascript/guides/nestjs/index.mdx:5-9` sets `fallbackGuide: javascript.node` with `server-node` categories and carries no runtime caveat. `docs/platforms/javascript/guides/cloudflare/frameworks/` has no `nestjs.mdx`, the framework list at `guides/cloudflare/index.mdx:16-23` omits Nest, and neither `httpServerHandler` nor `sentryOrchestrionPlugin` appears anywhere under `docs/` or `platform-includes/`.

Verified on the develop line: NestJS 11 with `@nestjs/platform-express` on workerd, `compatibility_date: "2026-05-01"`, `compatibility_flags: ["nodejs_compat", "enable_nodejs_http_modules", "enable_nodejs_http_server_modules"]`, served through `httpServerHandler` from `cloudflare:node` and wrapped with `Sentry.withSentry()` from `@sentry/cloudflare`. Nest must be booted lazily inside `fetch`, not at module scope. Bundle the Worker with esbuild plus `sentryOrchestrionPlugin()` from `@sentry/server-utils/orchestrion/esbuild` and point wrangler's `main` at the output. Error capture uses exactly the Node wiring: install `@sentry/nestjs` next to `@sentry/cloudflare` and register `SentryModule.forRoot()` in `imports` and `SentryGlobalFilter` from `@sentry/nestjs/setup` as an `APP_FILTER` provider.

Works today:

- Unhandled controller errors through `SentryGlobalFilter`, parameterized route names with middleware and handler spans, per-request isolation, incoming trace continuation, outgoing `fetch` spans with propagation headers, `db` spans, `Sentry.logger` logs.

Known limits:

- Plain `wrangler` bundling is not enough: without the orchestrion esbuild build there are no route names and no database spans.
- `mysql2` on workerd needs `disableEval: true` in the connection options, and the connection must be opened inside the request handler rather than at module scope.

What to change:

- Put the `disableEval` and connection-placement rules in a shared Workers database-driver note.
