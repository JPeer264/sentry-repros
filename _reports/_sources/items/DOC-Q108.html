DOC-Q108The Ember tracing pages document options that do not exist and omit the initializer tracing needsP1proven

Proposed issue docs-change

Rewrite the Ember tracing setup around the instance initializer and its real options

Description

guides/ember/configuration/ember-options.mdx:19-37 and :58-90 set Ember-specific options through ENV["@sentry/ember"] (disablePerformance, disableRunloopPerformance, disableInstrumentComponents, the minimum*Duration options), platform-includes/performance/enable-tracing/javascript.ember.mdx:1 says tracing is enabled by default and sideloaded as a chunk, and enable-automatic-instrumentation/javascript.ember.mdx:1-21 passes tracing options through a browserTracingOptions init option. The v2 addon reads no ENV section, exports no disablePerformance, and neither its declarations nor the browser options have browserTracingOptions. With the guide's init (tracesSampleRate: 1.0) no BrowserTracing integration is installed: no pageload or navigation span, no trace headers on the outgoing fetch, and the route instrumentation spans are dropped as no_parent_span. Tracing works after adding app/instance-initializers/sentry-performance.js that calls instrumentAppInstancePerformance(appInstance, {...}), documented only in the package's UPGRADE.md and README: route-named pageload and navigation segments (route:user with url.template /users/:user_id), router and hook spans, runloop tasks, web vitals, and propagated fetches. Its options (disableRunloopPerformance, minimumRunloopQueueDuration, disableInstrumentComponents, minimumComponentRenderDuration, enableComponentDefinitions, disableInitialLoadInstrumentation, instrumentPageLoad, instrumentNavigation) are documented nowhere. The same pages and enable-stream-mode/javascript.ember.mdx:5-27 speak of transactions and of opting in to stream mode with spanStreamingIntegration(); every root arrives as a span item and the client installs SpanStreaming by itself.

What to change:

  • Replace every ENV["@sentry/ember"] snippet and the browserTracingOptions snippet with the instance-initializer recipe and its options table, state that tracing is opt-in through that initializer, and describe segments with stream as the default.
