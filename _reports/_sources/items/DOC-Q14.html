Scope the built-in denylist claim on the data-collection include to request data

Description

platform-includes/getting-started-data-collection/javascript.mdx:8 says the SDK always filters sensitive values whose keys match a built-in denylist, such as auth or password, and sends [Filtered] instead.

Observed after the Redux step of the React guide (includes/platforms/configuration/integrations/redux.mdx:39-113, platform-includes/configuration/redux-init/javascript.mdx:1-21): contexts.state.state.value.password, the redux_state.json attachment and the redux.action breadcrumb data all carry password: "hunter2" in clear on every error event. The denylist applies to the request-data categories that dataCollection covers, not to state the app attaches.

What to change:

  • Scope the sentence to the request data the dataCollection categories cover.
  • On the Redux page, point at stateTransformer and actionTransformer for secrets in state.
