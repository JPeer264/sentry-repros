Proposed issue docs-change

Document Cloudflare Workflows step error capture on the final attempt

Description

docs/platforms/javascript/guides/cloudflare/features/workflows.mdx is 56 lines and covers trace_id derivation from instanceId (:11-15), the sampling decision (:17-23) and a warning about creating spans outside step.do() (:25-30). It never says what happens to an error thrown inside a step, so a reader cannot tell whether a retried failure appears once or many times.

On develop, packages/cloudflare/src/workflows.ts:152-153 (:151-153 at the pin; :150 is the catch) calls captureException only when isFinalAttempt, with mechanism: { handled: true, type: 'auto.faas.cloudflare.workflow' }, and then rethrows at :154, so the engine still sees the failure and retries. isFinalAttempt is computed at :114-121 on develop (:113-120 at the pin) as !hasStepContext || attempt > retryLimit: when the Workflows engine passes both attempt and config.retries.limit, only the attempt past the limit reports and intermediate retries send nothing; when no step context is supplied, every attempt is captured.

What to change

  • Add a section on step error capture to workflows.mdx.
  • State that a failing step.do() reports once, on the final attempt, where the engine supplies attempt and config.retries.limit, and that every attempt reports where it does not.
  • Name the mechanism auto.faas.cloudflare.workflow and say the error is rethrown, so retries and the workflow's own failure handling are unchanged.
