Linear SDK-1482 (Document automatic framework error capture in docs) removes setupExpressErrorHandler, setupFastifyErrorHandler, setupKoaErrorHandler and setupHapiErrorHandler from the four getting-started-use includes and points at shouldHandleError on expressIntegration() and fastifyIntegration(). No separate docs issue. Observed on develop: the four exports are deprecated and capture is identical without them on Express 4 and 5, Fastify 4 and 5, Koa and Hapi (QS-express, QS-fastify, QS-koa and QS-hapi, the no-handler variants); the Koa call also replaces Koa's default stderr error logging with a silent listener. The one exception, Koa with a direct first-line import, is DOC-Q45.
